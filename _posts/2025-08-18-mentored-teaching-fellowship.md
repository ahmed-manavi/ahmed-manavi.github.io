I was honored to be selected for the **2025 Mentored Teaching Fellowship**, sponsored by the NC State College of Engineering. Under the mentorship of my advisor, **Dr. Ali Cafer Gurbuz**, I provided teaching assistance for **ECE 301 &ndash; Linear Systems** throughout the Fall 2025 semester.

The fellowship paired me with a faculty mentor to develop hands-on teaching experience, from course design and interactive tools to direct student support, capped off with a joint reflection paper on the experience.
