I'm excited to return to **Bose Corporation** in Framingham, MA as a **Machine Learning and DSP Research Engineering Intern**, following my Audio ML & DSP Research Co-op there in 2024.

This time around, I'm working on **blind room geometry estimation** from ambient multi-microphone audio, building deep learning models (CRNNs) on spectral, phase, and spatial features, alongside a large-scale synthetic RIR data pipeline to support training and evaluation.

Looking forward to another round of great collaboration with the Audio Systems Research team!
